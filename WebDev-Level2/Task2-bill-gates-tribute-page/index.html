HIS JOURNEY

About Bill Gates

Discover the journey of a visionary who transformed technology.

Bill Gates was born on October 28, 1955, in Seattle, Washington, United States. From an early age, he demonstrated a strong interest in computers and technology. His curiosity and passion for problem-solving helped shape his future as one of the world's most influential technology entrepreneurs.

In 1973, Gates enrolled at Harvard University, where he studied mathematics and computer science. However, his growing interest in software development led him to leave university in 1975 and pursue a business opportunity with his childhood friend Paul Allen.

In 1975, Gates and Paul Allen founded Microsoft. Their vision was to make computers more accessible and useful for people around the world. Under Gates' leadership, Microsoft became a major technology company, particularly through the development of the Windows operating system.

Beyond technology, Gates became widely involved in philanthropy. Through the Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation, he has supported global health, education, and efforts to reduce poverty. His journey demonstrates how innovation and resources can be used to address important global challenges.
